Sakan Safety Guide

A few habits that protect you, whether you're renting or listing a place.

🔍 Verify before you commit. Every owner on Sakan has their WhatsApp number personally confirmed by our team before their listing goes live — but always view the property in person and confirm the owner's identity yourself before paying anything.
💳 Never pay before viewing. No deposit, no "booking fee," no exceptions — not until you've seen the place and met the owner face to face.
📞 Keep contact inside Sakan at first. Chat through the app until you're comfortable — phone numbers are only shared once the owner chooses to reveal them.
🧾 Get it in writing. Once you agree on terms, ask for a simple written agreement covering rent, deposit, duration, and what's included (utilities, furniture, etc.).
🚩 Trust your instincts. Pressure to move fast, requests for money via unusual methods, or refusal to meet in person are red flags — use the "Report this listing" link if something feels off.

Your first 90 days in Kuwait — Move-in Checklist

Bring these and ask these before you sign anything.

📋 Bring with you
  • Civil ID or passport copy
  • Proof of income / employer letter (some owners ask for this)
  • First month's rent + security deposit, in cash or KNET
  • A friend, colleague, or someone from your community if you can — a second set of eyes helps
❓ Ask the owner
  • Is the deposit refundable, and under what conditions?
  • What's included — water, electricity, internet, furniture?
  • Is subletting or having guests stay over allowed?
  • What's the process if something breaks (AC, water heater, etc.)?
  • How much notice is needed before moving out?

Why I built Sakan

A note from the founder.

I've lived the search for a room in Kuwait as an expat myself — scrolling through Facebook groups full of duplicate posts, chasing people on WhatsApp who never reply, and never being quite sure if the person on the other end of a listing is even real. It's stressful in a way it shouldn't have to be, especially when you've just arrived and everything else already feels unfamiliar.

Sakan started because I wanted something better than that for people in the same position I've been in. Not another noisy group chat or classifieds board — a place where every owner is actually verified before they can post, where you can message someone safely before ever sharing your number, and where finding a place doesn't mean gambling on a stranger's word.

This is still early days. I'm building and running Sakan myself, checking every listing personally before it goes live, because I'd rather grow slowly with something trustworthy than fast with something that isn't. If you're searching for a place, or have one to offer, I hope Sakan makes that a little easier — and a lot safer.

— The Sakan team

Privacy & how your data is used

Plain language, not legal boilerplate. Last updated August 2026.

What we collect. Your name, email, and phone number when you sign up or post a listing. Chat messages you send through Sakan are stored so conversations stay accessible to both sides.
Why we collect it. Before a listing goes live, our team personally confirms the owner's WhatsApp number is real and reachable — this is what lets Sakan verify owners when most platforms don't. Your contact details let other users reach you once you choose to share them; your phone number specifically is never shown to anyone until you personally tap "Share my number" in a chat.
Who reviews your details. Only Sakan Customer Care reviews listing and contact details — for the sole purpose of approving or rejecting a listing. Nothing is shown publicly beyond what you choose to include in your listing, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
Where it's stored. Sakan runs on Google Firebase, a widely-used, secure cloud infrastructure — the same kind of infrastructure banks and major apps run on. Photos are hosted via Cloudinary.
Your control over your data. You can ask to have your account and all your listings permanently removed at any time — just contact Sakan Customer Care. Deleting your account removes your profile and listings from Sakan.
Questions? Reach out any time through the contact details on the site.
